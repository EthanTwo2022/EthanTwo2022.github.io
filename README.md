## 👋 About Me

I'm a Master's student at Sun Yat-sen University (SYSU), working on **multi-sensor calibration** and **SLAM** at the RAPID Lab. My research focuses on developing robust and accurate calibration methods for autonomous systems.

## 🔬 Research Interests

- Multi-sensor Calibration (Camera, IMU, LiDAR)
- Visual-Inertial Odometry / SLAM
- Omnidirectional Vision
- Robotics & Autonomous Systems
